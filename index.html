About me

Hi! I'm a physical engineer from the Universidad Autónoma de Ciudad Juárez, in Mexico. I currently work in data analytics and data engineering, and I'm applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.

For my undergraduate thesis, advised by Dr. Jorge A. López at UTEP, I built an AI tutor for a university electromagnetism course. I designed the pipeline that generated its training data, fine-tuned the model, evaluated the data and deployed the tutor as a web app. A paper on this work is under review.

What stayed with me from that project is how hard it was to know when the model was right. My metrics could show that the data covered the course evenly, but not that a given answer was physically correct. I now want to work on models of physical systems that can tell you how much to trust them.

I'm interested in:

  • Scientific machine learning
  • Uncertainty quantification
  • Evaluating machine learning models, including language models

Implementation of an intelligent tutor based on large language models for university physics teaching
Jesús R. Berumen. Advisor: Jorge A. López. Co-advisor: Gildardo Rivas.
Undergraduate thesis, Universidad Autónoma de Ciudad Juárez, May 2025. Original title in Spanish: Implementación de un tutor inteligente basado en modelos de lenguaje de gran escala para la enseñanza de física universitaria.

A tutor for Principles of Electromagnetism (PHYS 2321) at UTEP. I wrote three kinds of seed examples (worked problems, conceptual questions and corrections of common mistakes) and expanded them into about 610 tutoring conversations with a curriculum-driven method based on Self-Instruct and Evol-Instruct. I fine-tuned GPT-4.1-mini on that data and deployed the tutor with a Flask backend and a PostgreSQL database. The generated data covered the course evenly (normalized topic entropy of 0.997), and the whole pipeline cost US$39.12.
